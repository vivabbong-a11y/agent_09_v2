"Hey Siri, what did Mom suggest for the weekend?" Siri can now take a question like this in Korean.

With the iOS 27.2 update (released October 27, 2026, Korea time), Apple's new **Siri AI** now supports Korean. One thing to know up front: **Siri AI is still a beta.** Some answers may be wrong, and how it behaves can change with updates.

This post covers what changed, how to turn it on, 10 ways to use it right away, and its current limits.

## 1. What changed?

The old Siri mostly handled one command at a time: timers, calls, weather. Apple describes Siri AI as **"an entirely new version of Siri"** built on Apple Intelligence.

> - Personal context: finds things in your messages, mail and photos
> - On-screen awareness: understands what is on your screen right now
> - Actions inside apps: does the task instead of just answering (including some third-party apps)
> - Broad knowledge Q&A: general questions with natural follow-ups
> - Camera "Siri mode" (Visual Intelligence) and writing tools
> - Conversation history: syncs privately across your devices through iCloud

In other words, Siri has moved from an assistant that answers to one that does the work. That is exactly the "AI agent" idea we covered in our earlier post, "What Is an AI Agent? Differences from Chatbots, How It Works, and Use Cases."

According to Apple's announcement, the new Apple Foundation Model behind it was custom-developed **"based on a collaboration with Google and Gemini models."**

Korean launched together with French, Japanese, Portuguese and Spanish, so Siri AI now supports six languages including English. Apple says more languages are coming by the end of the year.

## 2. How to turn it on and who can use it

### Step 1. Update to iOS 27.2
Settings > General > Software Update, then install iOS 27.2.

### Step 2. Match your languages
Your **device language and Siri language must both be Korean.**
- Device: Settings > General > Language & Region
- Siri: Settings > Siri > Language

### Step 3. Turn on Siri AI (join the waitlist)
Per Apple Support: Settings > **Siri** > **"Try Siri AI (Beta)"**. Choose a voice, complete voice enrollment and review the privacy settings. This puts you on a **waitlist**, and Siri AI downloads automatically when your turn comes.

> - The Korean menu name is unconfirmed (a Korean blog reports "Siri AI(베타) 체험"). **Menu wording may differ by device and version**, and some guides point to "Apple Intelligence & Siri" instead of "Siri."
> - Apple gives **no fixed waiting time.** Wi-Fi, a charger and enough free storage are recommended for the download.
> - Until you opt in, the old Siri stays active even after updating.

### Who can use it
> - **Device:** an Apple Intelligence-capable device. Instead of listing models here, check Apple's official pages:
>   - How to get Siri AI: https://support.apple.com/ko-kr/127893
>   - 차세대 Apple Intelligence 시작하기 (Getting started with next-generation Apple Intelligence): https://support.apple.com/ko-kr/121115
> - **Age:** 13 or older
> - **Region:** the Apple Account must be in an eligible region (not available on iOS/iPadOS/watchOS in the EU or with mainland China accounts)
> - **Storage:** up to 8 GB (up to 14 GB on some higher-end devices)
> - **Usage limits:** free for now, but server-based features have limits (details in section 4)

### How to call it
On iPhone: say "Siri야", press the side button, or swipe down from the Dynamic Island.

## 3. 10 ways to use it right away

Each one below is a **scenario described by Apple's announcement or the press.** The Korean commands are our wording, labelled **예시 (example)**, and were **not tested on a real device.**

**① Search your messages**
> 예시: "주말에 엄마가 뭐 하자고 했지?"

Siri finds the answer in your messages. (Basis: Apple Korea Newsroom example)

**② Mail → Reminders in one go**
> 예시: "할머니가 메일로 보낸 호박죽 재료, 장보기 목록에 넣어 줘"

It takes ingredients from an email and adds them to a Reminders shopping list. (Basis: scenario reported from Apple's release)

**③ Photo search + follow-up**
> 예시: "작년 제주도에서 찍은 가족사진 찾아 줘" → "그중에 바다 나온 사진만 보여 줘"

Narrow results with a follow-up question. (Basis: photo search is in Apple's announcement; wording from a Korean guide)

**④ Screen → Calendar**
> 예시: (on a friend's wedding website) "이 결혼식 일정, 장소까지 넣어서 캘린더에 추가해 줘"

It reads what is on screen and creates the event. (Basis: Apple's announcement)

**⑤ Camera Siri mode (Visual Intelligence)**
> 예시: (pointing the camera at a festival poster) "내가 좋아하는 아티스트 공연 시간 언제야? 캘린더에 넣어 줘"

(Basis: Apple's announcement)

**⑥ Draft writing, then revise by voice**
> 예시: "팀장님께 내일 회의를 30분 미뤄 달라는 메일 초안 써 줘" → "조금 더 정중하게 고쳐 줘"

(Basis: drafting and spoken revisions are in Apple's announcement; command is our wording)

**⑦ Add tasks in third-party apps**
> 예시: "Todoist에 '보고서 제출' 할 일 추가해 줘"

Apple names WhatsApp, Audible, Craft, Notability and Todoist. **Korean apps such as KakaoTalk or Naver were not mentioned.**

**⑧ General questions + follow-ups**
> 예시: "아이들이랑 걷기 좋은 숲길 추천해 줘" → "그 길 얼마나 길어? 아이들이 걷기에 무리 없어?"

(Basis: Apple Korea Newsroom example)

**⑨ Check meeting attendees (new in 27.2)**
> 예시: "다음 주 회의 참석자가 누구야?"

Calendar attendees became a data source in 27.2. (Basis: MacRumors; wording from a Korean guide)

**⑩ Continue on another device**
> 예시: Ask on iPhone, then continue the same conversation in the Siri app on Mac or iPad

History syncs privately through iCloud. (Basis: Apple's announcement)

> **Tip:** Because it is a beta, double-check dates, times and amounts against the original message or mail.

## 4. Current limits and privacy

[Image 4: Smartphone showing a lock icon (illustrative). Photo by Dan Nelson on Unsplash, https://unsplash.com/photos/smartphone-with-security-lock-icon-ah-HeguOe9k]

### Current limits
> - **Beta:** answers can be wrong, and features can change.
> - **Usage limits:** server-based features such as Siri AI have limits that vary "by feature, request complexity, system demand" and more. When you hit one, that feature returns after a time-out period. Apple has **not published the exact amount or reset time.**
> - **Paid tier:** Apple says expanded access "will be available for a fee" in the future. **No price or date has been announced.**
> - **Korean apps:** support for KakaoTalk, Naver and other Korean apps is not documented.
> - **No hands-on reviews yet:** we found no in-depth review of Korean speech-recognition quality.

### Privacy
> - Requests run **on device** when possible, and on Apple's **Private Cloud Compute** only when needed.
> - Apple says data processed in Private Cloud Compute is **not stored or made accessible to anyone, Apple included**, and independent experts can verify this.
> - During setup you can decline the optional sharing of audio and text with Apple.
> - iOS 27.2 adds a setting to exclude apps that Siri, Search and suggestions can access. The Korean menu name is unconfirmed and **may differ by device.**

## 5. Wrap-up

> **Summary**
> - Siri AI supports Korean from iOS 27.2 (**beta**)
> - Turn it on: Settings > Siri > Try Siri AI (Beta), then join the waitlist (menu wording may differ)
> - Requirements: eligible device (see Apple's support pages), 13+, eligible region, matching device and Siri language
> - Main uses: search messages, mail and photos, on-screen actions, camera mode, writing help, third-party apps, follow-up questions
> - Free for now with usage limits; paid tier price not announced

Siri AI is the first step of an "AI agent" moving into your phone. Since it is a beta, start with simple things like finding photos or adding calendar events, and always double-check important details. We will update this post once we have tried it on a real device.
