## Card 2 — Hook / Intro

- **Title**: Same pen, different answers
- **Copy**:
  - The "best pen" depends on what you write.
  - Fast notes? Clear colors? Water resistance?
  - Start with the three ink types.
- **Info box (ink types)**:
  - **Oil-based**: dries fast, smudges little, water resistant
  - **Gel**: vivid lines with light pressure
  - **Water-based**: almost no pressure needed, but weak against water
- **Visual**: Three ink drops in different colors with icons
- **Source**: Vegan News; ajd ballpoint guide

## Card 3 — Students ① Study Notes

- **Title**: For students who write all day
- **Recommendations**:
  - **Jetstream 0.5mm** — low-viscosity oil ink, quick to dry; often ranked first among note-taking pens
  - **Sarasa / Signo** — gel pens frequently mentioned by students; good for color-coding key points
- **Tip**: Pick one main color and only 1–2 highlight colors — too many colors slow you down.
- **Visual**: An open notebook with black text and two accent colors
- **Source**: nosearch note-taking pen ranking; online community posts

## Card 4 — Students ② Exam Day

- **Title**: On CSAT day, the pen is (mostly) given to you
- **Copy**:
  - The computer-use sign pen and the mechanical pencil are **handed out on site**.
  - The mechanical pencil **must be the one provided**.
  - Your own sign pen and leads may be used.
- **Fun fact**: The mint-colored 2024 CSAT mechanical pencil was resold for about 10,000 won the day after the exam.
- **Caution note**: "Always check the latest official exam guidelines."
- **Visual**: Exam-day checklist with check boxes
- **Source**: Naeil Education Q&A (2025); Malay Mail (2024)

## Card 5 — Office Workers

- **Title**: One pen for meetings, one for signatures
- **Recommendations**:
  - **Jetstream 4&1** — four colors plus a mechanical pencil in one body; a best item on Kyobo Hottracks
  - **Monami 153** — the "national ballpoint" since the 1960s; oil ink suits documents
  - **LAMY Safari** — a popular premium pick, good as a gift
- **Tip**: Oil-based ink for contracts and documents that must not smudge.
- **Visual**: Office desk with a meeting notebook, a contract and three pens
- **Source**: Kyobo Hottracks writing-instrument pages; Hankyung (2014)

## Card 6 — Journaling (Dakku) Fans

- **Title**: Pens that make your diary page shine
- **Recommendations**:
  - **Pilot Juice Up multi-color** — multi-color pen featured as a best item on Hottracks
  - **Sarasa / Signo** — wide color range for lettering and doodles
  - **Monami 153 limited editions** — collectible designs such as "Black Bunny"
- **Tip**: Water-based ink can bleed with water or stickers on top — test first.
- **Visual**: Decorated diary page with stickers and colorful lettering
- **Source**: Kyobo Hottracks multi-pen page; Careet

## Card 7 — Summary

- **Title**: Life pens at a glance
- **Table**:

| Situation | Pen | Why |
|-----------|-----|-----|
| Study notes | Jetstream 0.5 | Fast drying, little smudging |
| Color-coding | Sarasa · Signo | Vivid gel colors |
| Exam day | Provided items | Pencil must be the one provided |
| Meetings | Jetstream 4&1 | Four colors + pencil in one |
| Documents | Monami 153 | Oil ink, classic |
| Diary decorating | Juice Up · limited editions | Colors and design |

- **Visual**: Clean infographic table with situation icons
