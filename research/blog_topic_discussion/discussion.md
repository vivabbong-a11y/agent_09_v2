## Round 3: research-agent fact-check and pushback

**Siri AI in Korean**
- Release: iOS 27.2 (along with the other 27.2 operating systems), announced Oct 26 US time. Confirmed by fnnews and Wikitree (2026-10-27), both quoting Apple's release.
- Limits: still labelled **beta**. Server-side features have daily usage limits, and a paid option to raise them will come later (price not announced). Not available to users under 13.
- Devices: described only as "Apple Intelligence-capable devices." The "iPhone 15 Pro and later" cut-off is unconfirmed, so the post should link Apple's support page instead of listing models.
- Gemini: reported via Apple's release ("with Google and Gemini models").
- Working now: searching messages, mail and photos; on-screen actions; camera lookups; writing help; tasks across apps; follow-up questions; iCloud-synced history.

**Sale festival**
- No official ministry press release was found.
- Regional media say Oct 29 – Nov 15, but the opening-ceremony date conflicts (Oct 29 vs Oct 31). Sourcing is **thin**.

**Kimjang figures**
- Source: YTN, 2026-10-08, prices as of Oct 6. YTN does not name its data source.
- KAMIS or the aT survey (due mid-November) is the better anchor.

**Pushback**
- Drop the festival half and narrow the merged post to "김장 장보기, AI에게 맡겨봤다".
