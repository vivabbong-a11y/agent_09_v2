## 2. Book Summary

- Explains the AI industry ecosystem for general readers who need to make decisions about business, careers or investing. The focus is how the industry is structured, not stock picks or short-term forecasts.
- Follows the money from a single user prompt through the AI value chain. The argument is that capital flows first to bottlenecks in that chain.
- Main topics:
  - Nvidia's lock-in through the CUDA software ecosystem
  - Hardware and infrastructure winners such as SK hynix and TSMC
  - Data centers, power, cooling, space, crypto and tokens
  - Autonomous AI agents
  - "Physical AI", where robots' labor output is sold as a service
- Marketing points: the author has 30 years in the Korean IT industry and writes the *IT Trend* series. The book also covers semiconductors, AX, power, crypto and space, and analyzes key global and Korean AI companies for 2027.
